Shipping & Returns

1. Shipping

1. Delivery conditions

Nostalgia delivers to Greece and selected international destinations. Delivery is initiated after successful payment confirmation.

Estimated transit times are business days and start from shipment date:

Country / RegionEstimated time
Greece (mainland)1 to 3 days
Greek islands2 to 5 days
EU destinations3 to 7 days
United Kingdom5 to 8 days

Once shipped, you receive a tracking number by email to monitor your parcel in real time.

2. Delivery costs

Delivery costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and shipping method.

DestinationShipping fee
Greece10€
EU Zone A15€
EU Zone B18€
Switzerland35€
United Kingdom£18

For eligible orders above 100€ within the EU, delivery may be free depending on the selected method.

3. Receipt of goods

At delivery, please verify package condition and order conformity. If visible damage is present, note reservations with the carrier and contact us promptly through the contact page.

4. Returning your product

You may request withdrawal within fourteen (14) days from delivery. Returned items must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging with labels and related documentation.

If return conditions are not met, reimbursement may be refused and the item can be sent back to the provided customer address.

5. Reimbursement and customer services

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within up to fourteen (14) days from return receipt or proof of shipment.

For any claim regarding delivery, damage, replacement, or refund status, please contact our team through the contact form.
