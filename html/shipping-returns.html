1. Shipping

1. Delivery conditions

Nostalgia delivers to Greece and selected international destinations. Delivery is initiated after successful payment confirmation.

Estimated transit times are business days and start from shipment date:

Country / RegionEstimated time
Greece (mainland)1 to 3 days
Greek islands2 to 5 days
EU destinations3 to 7 days
United Kingdom5 to 8 days

Once shipped, you receive a tracking number by email to monitor your parcel in real time.

3. Receipt of goods

At delivery, please verify package condition and order conformity. If visible damage is present, note reservations with the carrier and contact us promptly through the contact page.

4. Returning your product

You may request withdrawal within fourteen (14) days from delivery. Returned items must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging with labels and related documentation.

If return conditions are not met, reimbursement may be refused and the item can be sent back to the provided customer address.

Full steps and a model notice are in the Cancellation and Withdrawal Policy.

5. Reimbursement and customer services

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within up to fourteen (14) days from return receipt or proof of shipment.

For any claim regarding delivery, damage, replacement, or refund status, please contact our team through the contact form.

6. Non-receipt or lost parcel

If an order is not delivered within a reasonable time after shipment, we investigate the shipment together with the carrier using the tracking number. If the parcel is confirmed lost in transit, we resend the order or fully refund it, at no additional charge to you.

Please report a non-delivered order promptly through the contact page.

7. Defective or damaged products

If a product arrives defective, damaged in transit, or not matching your order, you are entitled to a replacement or a full refund at no extra cost. Please contact us within fourteen (14) days of receipt, ideally with a photo of the item and the packaging.

This does not affect your statutory rights under consumer protection law. Minor variations in colour, shape and texture in handmade items are not considered defects.

Full guarantee policy: Guarantees and Faulty Products.

8. Order cancellation

You may cancel your order free of charge as long as it has not yet been shipped. In that case the full amount is refunded to the original payment method.

To cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible through the contact page. Once an order has shipped, the right of withdrawal (section 4) applies.
